# ADR 0001 – Record architecture decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-02

## Context
The PAS is built mainly with AI assistance and will later be handed to a human developer. Future maintainers need to know why decisions were made.

## Decision
Record each significant architecture decision as a short, numbered Architecture Decision Record (ADR) in `docs/adr/`, covering context, decision and consequences.

## Consequences
Decisions are traceable. Superseded ADRs are marked rather than deleted.
