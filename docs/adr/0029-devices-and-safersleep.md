# ADR 0029 – Devices & SaferSleep (bedside monitors and the anaesthetic record)

Status: Accepted (0.17.0 – foundations; device and vendor profiles to be confirmed on site)

## Context
Spec 4.7 / 6 / 7.6 ask for vital signs from the GE CARESCAPE monitors (B450 in theatre, B1x5 in recovery) to reach the observation chart without retyping, and for the SaferSleep anaesthetic record to be part of the patient's record. Both are clinical-safety interfaces: a reading charted against the wrong patient, or an artefact charted as an observation, is worse than no interface. The built-in messaging engine (ADR 0021) already listens for HL7 over MLLP with allowed-client lists, stores every message and acknowledges it; the equipment register (ADR 0025) already holds the monitors.

## Decision
- New module `devices` (schema `devices`), licensed separately, built on the messaging engine: two new inbound handlers (`DeviceObservations`, `AnaesthesiaRecord`) and one outbound interface. No device talks to the module directly, so allowed IP lists, message storage, retries and the message viewer are the engine's.
- **Monitors** are equipment-register rows plus a `Monitor` row holding what the gateway calls them (bed label from PV1-3 such as `PACU/BAY1`, or the equipment id in OBX-18). A message from a monitor BrightPAS does not know is **rejected** (MSA|AR) so a gateway set-up fault is visible rather than silently stored.
- **Monitor results** are HL7 ORU^R01 in the IHE PCD-01 style the CARESCAPE Gateway sends: IEEE 11073 (MDC) codes in OBX-3, with plain labels accepted as well. Codes map to a small set (HR, PR, SpO2, NIBP, arterial pressure, RR, temperature, end-tidal CO2). Units are normalised (°F → °C, kPa → mmHg). Values outside plausibility limits are discarded as artefact. Readings are **downsampled** (default one per measurement per 30 s) – this is a chart feed, not waveform storage.
- **Patient–monitor connection is explicit.** A nurse chooses the monitor on the episode and sees its live values before confirming. One patient per monitor and one monitor per patient at a time is enforced by **filtered unique indexes**, not only by code. Connecting a second monitor (theatre → recovery) ends the first in the same transaction. If the monitor is sending a different NHI (patient admitted on the monitor), connection is refused. Optional auto-connect when the monitor sends the NHI of a patient who is admitted. Connections end automatically when the patient is discharged.
- **Nothing reaches the observation chart unvalidated.** A background worker offers a *capture* per connected patient at an interval by stage (theatre 5, recovery 5, ward 15 min by default). A nurse ticks which values to accept, adds what only a person can assess (consciousness, pain, oxygen, sedation) and validates – this creates a normal eMR observation with `Source = Device` and the early warning score – or rejects it with a reason. Rejected captures are kept for audit (reporting view `vDeviceCapture`).
- **SaferSleep out:** ADT^A01 on admission (or on entering theatre – setting), A08 on request, A03 on discharge, queued on a named outbound interface. PV1-19 carries the episode id as the **visit number**. Allergies recorded in BrightPAS are sent as AL1; when none is recorded **no AL1 is sent** – BrightPAS knowing of no allergy is not the same as "no known allergies".
- **SaferSleep in:** the completed record as ORU^R01 – OBR-3 case number, OBR-25 F/C, header OBX (ASA, technique, airway, comments), `DRUG`/`DRUGINF` OBX with time, dose, unit and route, `EVENT` OBX, MDC-coded vital signs, and the PDF as OBX ED (Base64, checked to be a PDF). Matching is by visit number, else NHI plus a single admission that day. A visit number whose patient has a different NHI is **rejected**. Anything else unmatched is **kept unfiled** for a person to file (Devices › SaferSleep), offering only episodes of the same NHI.
- Filing a record adds the PDF to the patient's documents, a signed anaesthetic note, and (setting) the drugs to the eMR medicines list with `Source = SaferSleep`. A **correction** (same case number) supersedes the earlier record, amends the note and replaces the medicines; a **resent** message (same control id) is acknowledged and ignored. Controlled-drug register entries are never created from the interface.
- **Retention:** readings from a monitor with no patient connected are deleted after 24 h (setting); monitor HL7 messages in the message store after 48 h (failed ones after 7 days) – the readings themselves are the record.
- Permissions: `devices.use` (Nurse, Anaesthetist), `devices.manage` (System Administrator). Validating also needs `emr.write`.

## Addendum 0.19.1 – GE CARESCAPE Gateway Technical Manual (2052437-015, software 2.2)
- The gateway sends trends in PCD-01 form **or** GE's own form (Appendix E mnemonics such as HR, SPO2-%, NBP-S, AR1-S, CO2-EX, TP1-1; units "mm (hg)", "cel", "/min", "%"). `DeviceHl7` accepts both; slot-numbered pressures/temperatures are matched by pattern; a second SpO2 probe is ignored; CO2-EX in % uses the message's barometric pressure (AMB_P/PBAR) or 760 mmHg.
- The gateway is the TCP client for outbound HL7 (configured in Webmin › Cloverleaf Thread Startup with BrightPAS's address and port); it listens for ADT inbound (not used by BrightPAS yet – it would let monitors "Request Admit" by unit and bed).
- **Back-collection:** up to 8 h of data held by a monitor while offline is sent when it returns. The live ingest therefore de-duplicates against readings held around each reading's time (as the file import does) and files each reading to the connection active at that time.
- **Daylight saving:** the manual states that during fall-back the gateway repeats timestamps with new periodic data and may resend episodic data. A live timestamp without an offset in the repeated hour is resolved to the occurrence nearest the arrival time (`DeviceHl7.LiveTimestamp`).
- HIS mappings containing a caret arrive with it doubled; the device key ignores empty components.

## Consequences
- The HL7 shapes are BrightPAS **interface profiles** built from the published standards, not from TDS's actual gateway or SaferSleep output. Before go-live: capture real messages from the CARESCAPE Gateway (codes MDC vs GE's own, units, send interval, bed labels) and agree the record profile with SaferSleep; both are mapping changes in `DeviceHl7`, covered by tests.
- The test robot plays both systems (virtual GE gateway and virtual SaferSleep) so the whole path – screens included – is tested without hardware; it cannot prove behaviour of the real devices.
- No waveforms, alarms or alarm forwarding: BrightPAS is a record, not a monitoring or alarm system, and must not be relied on for either. The monitor and central station remain the clinical alarm source.
- MLLP is unencrypted on the clinical network (allowed-IP lists only) until MLLP over TLS is added to the engine (backlog).
- Chart intervals, plausibility limits and the validation workflow need TDS clinical governance sign-off with the early warning score bands (ADR 0026).
